This deck didn't do too well in the records, but I think it actually wasn't too far off from being a solid deck. I've had success with these kinds of decks in the past, but this one just had a few issues.

- Lacking counterspells. For a deck that wants to "protect the queen", I had very few ways to do that. 
- Lacking removal. I had trouble any time my opponent managed to stick some threats or build a board. I splashed black out of necessity - I just didn't see enough red removal. 

Wins were largely on the back of Harvester of Misery and Jace, the Mind Sculptor. 

Mischievous Mystic and Sprite Dragon were quite solid as well. I also really enjoyed playing with Fists of Flame.

This deck could have really used an Arc Trail or a Forked Bolt to generate some 2-for-1s (neither is currently present in the cube, and I missed out on fire // ice)
