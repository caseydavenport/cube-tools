Pack felt like he was sitting in a perfectly open aristocrats lane, and the deck shows. It's quite tight and did really well - it was fast and hard to deal with due to recursion. Even when I had Scrabbling Claws out it was a challenge to keep up. 

Pack reported that Goblin Bombardment and Moonshadow felt incredibly strong. 

Carrion Feeder was a challenge to play against, although Seal of Removal was a great answer here. 

Bloodghast and Bloodsoaked Champion did massive work against my deck (I didn't see Goblin Bombardment personally) but they were still great with cards like Bone Shards and Carrion Feeder, and just as recurrable threats that I didn't have an answer to and couldn't block productively.

I suspect the strength of this deck was amplified by the fact that we were only a 6-pod, but this wasn't the only Rakdos deck at the table so the strength of it is something to keep an eye on. Goblin Bombardment and Bitterblossom in particular can feel oppressive to play against since they are so hard to answer.
